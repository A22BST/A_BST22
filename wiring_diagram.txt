SIMON SAYS WIRING DIAGRAM
========================

Power Supply (RS PRO LI60-20B24PR2)
├── +24V ──────────────────── +24V Terminal Block
│                               ├── Red Button Pin 1 (Brown)
│                               ├── Blue Button Pin 1 (Brown)
│                               └── Yellow Button Pin 1 (Brown)
│
└── 0V ───────────────────── 0V Terminal Block
                                ├── Red Button Pin 3 (Blue)
                                ├── Blue Button Pin 3 (Blue)
                                └── Yellow Button Pin 3 (Blue)

PLC Digital Inputs (NX-ID4442)
├── DI1 ─────────────────── Yellow Button Pin 4 (Black)
├── DI3 ─────────────────── Blue Button Pin 4 (Black)
└── DI5 ─────────────────── Red Button Pin 4 (Black)

PLC Digital Outputs (NX-OD4256)
├── DO0 ─────────────────── Red Button Pin 2 (White)
├── DO1 ─────────────────── Blue Button Pin 2 (White)
└── DO2 ─────────────────── Yellow Button Pin 2 (White)

BUTTON DETAILS (RAFI RAMO 22 T - M12, 4-pin)
=============================================
Pin 1 (Brown)  = Contact Common (+24V)
Pin 2 (White)  = LED Positive (from PLC output)
Pin 3 (Blue)   = LED Negative (to 0V)
Pin 4 (Black)  = Contact Output (to PLC input)
